# US-001 — Sign in with credentials

## Statement
**As a** P-01 (senior technician), **I want to** sign in with username and password on the mobile device, **so that** my task list is mine and not shared with other technicians.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given invalid credentials, when I tap Sign in, then I see an inline error and stay on the sign-in screen.
